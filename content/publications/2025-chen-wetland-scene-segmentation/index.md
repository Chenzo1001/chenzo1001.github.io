In this paper, we propose a novel wetland scene segmentation method based on Lie group feature and graph cut model. The Lie group feature is extracted by the Lie group convolutional neural network (LGCNN), which can effectively extract the local structure information of wetland scenes. The graph cut model is used to optimize the segmentation results, which can effectively remove the noise and ensure the continuity of the segmentation results. The experimental results show that the proposed method can achieve better segmentation results than the traditional methods.
